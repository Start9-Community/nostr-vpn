Nostr VPN Support

Need help with Nostr VPN? Email nostrvpn@siriusbusiness.fi. No account is required to contact support.

Before contacting support

  1. Confirm that the VPN permission is enabled in system settings.
  2. Stop and restart the VPN from the Devices tab.
  3. If a peer cannot connect, confirm that both devices use the intended network and that its signed join request was approved.

What to include

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send private keys, wallet secrets, recovery material, or authentication credentials.

Privacy

Read the Nostr VPN Privacy Policy for details about VPN data and local storage.
